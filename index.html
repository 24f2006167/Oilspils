Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) • INCOIS • Indian Coast Guard (ICG)
MOSTA: Marine Oil Spill Tracing & Attribution Platform SIH26143

An end-to-end operational intelligence system designed to detect unannounced marine petroleum discharges across India's 2.37 million km² Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ), backtrack dispersion physics to exact origin coordinates, and attribute culpability to specific commercial ships using historical AIS trajectories and Machine Learning.

⚡ 5-Stage Physics & AI Attribution Pipeline
🛰️ 1. SAR AI Detection
Ingests Sentinel-1 C-Band / RISAT-1A SAR rasters. Applies Enhanced Lee speckle filtering, adaptive sea-clutter contrast thresholding, and contour vectorization to extract georeferenced slick polygons and area ($km^2$).
🌊 2. Lagrangian Backtrack
Numerically integrates reverse particle transport incorporating 3.2% windage drift, +10° Coriolis deflection, CMEMS/INCOIS ODAS surface current vectors, and turbulent Brownian eddy diffusion to compute release origin & time window.
🚢 3. AIS Trajectory Matching
Cross-filters historical Class-A AIS transponder feeds across origin envelope. Computes Great-Circle Haversine distance ($d_{\min}$), residence time, and flags operational speed drops ($\Delta v \ge 4\text{ kts}$).
🏆 4. Multi-Factor ML Rank
Fuses 5 weighted forensic factors (Proximity 30%, Time 25%, Behavior 20%, Drift 15%, AIS 10%) with trained XGBoost/LightGBM classifier to assign continuous culpability probabilities (0–100%).
⚖️ 5. Legal Enforcement
Auto-compiles official forensic attribution dossiers with SHA-256 evidence verification for Indian Coast Guard enforcement under Merchant Shipping Act (1958) Section 356C and MARPOL 73/78 Annex I.
🛢️ Oil Weathering Kinetics
Computes Mackay evaporative exposure ($F_v$), emulsification water uptake ($Y_w$), and dynamic viscosity evolution ($\mu$) across ambient sea surface temperatures ($28.4^\circ\text{C}$).
